## Grading Rubric

### 1. Content & Presentation — 25 Points

Please consider whether:

- Our AI topic is recent and relevant.
- The information is accurate and clearly explained.
- The video is organized and within the required time limit.
- All group members participated.
- The presentation ends with a good discussion question.

### 2. GitHub Collaboration — 25 Points

Please consider whether:

- The repository is organized.
- All group members made commits.
- We used GitHub Issues and branches.
- The README file is complete.
- We used the GitHub Discussion board.

### 3. AI Evaluation Setup — 25 Points

Please check whether:

- We used two different AI models.
- We saved the full prompts and responses.
- We included the grading rubric in our prompts.
- Our prompts were thoughtful and specific.

### 4. Reflection Quality — 25 Points

Please check whether:

- We summarized both AI evaluations.
- We compared the results from both AI models.
- We shared our honest opinion about the grades.
- We gave specific examples of how AI helped or did not help with our research.

## Please Include in Your Evaluation

1. A score out of 25 for each category.
2. A total grade out of 100.
3. A short explanation for each score.
4. Three strengths of our project.
5. Three areas we could improve.
6. Any other feedback that may help us.

Please be honest and base your evaluation only on what you can actually see in the GitHub repository and YouTube video. If you cannot access or verify something, please mention it instead of assuming it was completed.
